A mode is a full set of buttons inside this one layout, for example one for walking, one for driving and one for swimming. While you play, tap the round Modes button (beside the menu button) to open the list and pick a mode. Tap a mode's icon here, or tap the current mode (or hold any mode) in the editor's mode row, to give it a picture, such as a person or a car, or to delete it. Turn off “In switcher” for a mode you do not want in the list.

What is WebTroller?

WebTroller (Website Controller) puts a customizable on-screen game controller on top of any website. Its buttons send real key presses, mouse clicks, scrolls and mouse movement to the page, so you can play browser games and web emulators on a touch screen, or control any page without a keyboard and mouse.

On your phone or tablet
  • Tap Edit Layout to drag, resize and style buttons and analog sticks, or tap Controllers to start from a ready-made one.
  • Portrait and landscape each keep their own layout. Save layouts and load them again later.
  • Lock Screen stops accidental touches from clicking or scrolling the website under your controller.
  • Keyboard opens WebTroller's own keyboard so your phone's keyboard does not cover the game.
  • Visibility hides every controller button for a moment. Fullscreen, Opacity, Brightness and Volume help you play comfortably.
  • Performance and Network show FPS and ping while you play.
Ad Blocker (with AdGuard DNS)

One switch does it all. Ad Blocker blocks known ad networks, hides leftover ad boxes and stops pop-ups. It also uses AdGuard DNS: it asks AdGuard's DNS server whether the third-party servers a page talks to are ad or tracker servers, and blocks the ones it flags. Only those server names are sent (for example ads.example.com), never the page address, your typing or your passwords. It does not change the phone's DNS for other apps. It applies to pages and requests loaded after you turn it on.

Good to know
  • Tap the round ☰ button to open this menu. Tap outside a panel to close it.
  • WebTroller stays open on a tab through reloads until you hide it with the extension icon.
  • Works in Kiwi Browser and Firefox on Android, and in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox on a computer.
